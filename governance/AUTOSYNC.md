# Public profile reconciliation

Human authority remains final. Level 2 is accountable for the consistency and privacy of professional claims across the portfolio, GitHub, resume, Wix integrations, LinkedIn and Indeed.

`profile.json` is the sanitized public claims register. It points to the existing professional profile, credential catalogue, resume and dated engineering evidence. Domain records retain their original evidence dates. A new publication date never refreshes an old test or upgrades document review to issuer verification.

## Release procedure

1. Compare the current register with source artifacts and account-specific state. Reuse completed material. Resolve a discrepancy at its source and update all affected projections.
2. Preserve a recoverable revision. Use one writer and check the expected repository head before applying changes. Keep independent projects and accounts separate.
3. Validate privacy, local links, claim boundaries and the rendered resume. Never export private governance records, network endpoints, household data, passwords or access tokens.
4. Publish only within authenticated, delegated capabilities and applicable platform gates. Account setup, an installed integration and a public deployment are distinct states.
5. Verify the actual public URLs and content after publication. Record failed or unavailable destinations as unresolved. A workflow named sync is not proof of cross-platform synchronization.

Use See It–Own It–Solve It–Do It: record the defect, Level 2 owner, correction, postcheck, rollback revision and remaining gap. Review repeated content errors, process patterns and handoff failures. Missing evidence keeps the affected claim on HOLD while independent eligible work continues.

The existing GitHub portfolio remains the public source until a separate publishing destination is established. `integrations/wix-career-embed.html` is a generated, sanitized copy of the existing career portal for Wix integration; it is not evidence of a Wix publication. LinkedIn and Indeed account updates require their own observed receipts. No native runtime release or automatic dispatch is established by these documents.
