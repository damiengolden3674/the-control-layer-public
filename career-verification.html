LIVE VERIFICATION · CHECK THE TIMESTAMP

Governed systems and security evidence

Consult the public verification surface and software security state. A live result must include the source, observed time, scope, and a matching status.

If the live verifier is unavailable, outdated, or reports HOLD, do not treat historical values as current.

How to verify a claim

  1. Check the exact credential. Open its issuer-provided URL in the catalogue, compare the title and identity, and treat an unreachable link as unverified—not failed or verified.
  2. Check the precise engineering artifact. Review the public repository and its linked proof, including revision and test scope.
  3. Distinguish proof classes. Document-reviewed, issuer-verified, and live-runtime-verified are separate states. Neither internal engineering tests nor framework crosswalks create external credentials.
  4. Evaluate unresolved holds. Where component acceptance, privileged Human STOP recovery, app distribution or disaster-recovery proof is incomplete, the correct state is HOLD.
Verification policy: Intelligence is not authority. Human approval establishes permission to perform governed work, not independent evidence that an exam was passed, an account was verified, or a deployment met all acceptance criteria.
