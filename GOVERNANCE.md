# Public Share Governance

Hierarchy:

Human → Human-authorized AI architect/builder → The Control Layer → Control AI / AI orchestration → Model router / AI models → Governed executor → Operating ecosystem

Core rule: **Intelligence ≠ Authority.**

Delegated actions require verified access, explicit scope, and evidence. Human-only privileged actions remain with the human. Documented authority does not prove an active autonomous dispatcher. Reporting flows from the ecosystem through the hierarchy to the human Architect. The public share edition is a sanitized presentation surface and must never contain credentials, PII, private infrastructure identifiers, or private legal evidence.

Evidence states: VERIFIED, DOCUMENTED CLAIM, NOT VERIFIED.
