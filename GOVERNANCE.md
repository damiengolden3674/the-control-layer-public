# Public Share Governance

Hierarchy:

Human → Frontier AI Combination → The Control Layer → Autonomous Capability Fabric → Specialist AI Pool → Governed Executor → Ecosystem → Proof/Audit

Core rule: **Intelligence ≠ Authority.**

Autonomy is complete within an actor's assigned authority ceiling. Crossing the ceiling escalates upward. The public share edition is a sanitized presentation surface and must never contain credentials, PII, private infrastructure identifiers, or private legal evidence.

Evidence states: VERIFIED, DOCUMENTED CLAIM, NOT VERIFIED.
