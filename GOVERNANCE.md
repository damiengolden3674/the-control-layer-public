# The Control Layer™ — Public Governance Model

**Founder / Architect:** Damien Golden · **Organization:** Golden Governance LLC

**Principle: Intelligence is not authority.** This document describes the governance architecture and its evidence requirements, not a claim of full autonomous deployment.

## Authority and six-level accountability

1. **Human authority** — Permanent final decision authority. Native privileged approvals and other Human-only decisions are never issued by an AI.
2. **Level 2: AI leadership and architecture** — Receives authenticated Human instructions, applies Human-provided leadership methods, coordinates the ecosystem, delegates approved work, verifies outcomes, and reports directly to the Human.
3. **Level 3: Control Layer orchestration** — Policy gates, intake, dependencies, coordination, incident routing, and audit.
4. **Level 4: Model selection and AI advisers** — Task-fit model selection, bounded analysis, provenance, and data-scope controls; model output does not grant execution authority.
5. **Level 5: Governed executors** — Exact, authenticated, allowed actions with one owner/writer, postchecks, and recovery evidence.
6. **Level 6: Operating ecosystem** — Devices, applications, integrations, storage, networking, security, monitoring, and governed operational state.

Levels 2–6 are designed to inspect the quality of adjacent handoffs and report defects upward. Shared reasoning methods do not grant equal tool permissions, model weights, unrestricted private data, or Human authority.

## Scope and third-party services

Governance covers the use of AI systems such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and local models, along with GitHub, connected tools, software, and other third-party applications **only within authenticated, supported, delegated capabilities**. A policy document cannot change a vendor account, establish cross-device synchronization, or install an application by itself.

## Incident and recovery controls

- **EXECUTE:** Verified reversible work inside established authority, resource, privacy, ownership, and recovery limits.
- **HOLD:** Missing authority, stale or conflicting evidence, unknown scope, or failed safety prerequisites; continue unrelated eligible work.
- **ESCALATE:** Critical incidents and problems requiring Level 2 intervention trigger a priority path to Human authority. This is not automatic Human approval. Human-only, destructive, privilege-expanding, and high-risk actions retain their gates.
- **VERIFY:** Record the source observation time, accountable owner, implementation and independent postcheck, negative tests, rollback evidence, and remaining gaps.

Operational improvement follows **See It–Own It–Solve It–Do It**, Content/Pattern/Relationship accountability, measurable corrective closure, and leadership review.

## Project intelligence and continuous improvement

Level 2 may share sanitized, role-scoped governance competencies downward while preserving project identity and private personnel data. Each level's work must be judged by observed results, verified skills, bounded resources, and recovery—not a simulated proficiency score.

An S-Tier (A+++) goal is an **internal engineering acceptance target**, not an external credential, guaranteed score, or evidence that all models or devices are operating. Source-code commits, tests, and reference policies must be distinguished from authenticated deployments and end-to-end acceptance.

## Current evidence boundary

Public portfolio artifacts demonstrate reviewed design and isolated engineering tests. Full ecosystem activation, live cross-provider AI participation, mobile resynchronization, private-storage migration, and disaster recovery acceptance require separate current execution and independent verification.

This public surface intentionally excludes credentials, internal IP addresses, private project records, personnel information, and sensitive infrastructure configuration.
